Mathematical formulation of continuous $(P, \dot{m})$ state phase-plane monitoring inside NASA EMU / xEVAS Portable Life Support Systems (PLSS).

1. Latent Heat & Phase Change Equilibrium

Feedwater at pressure $P$ reaches sintered micro-pores exposed to space vacuum ($P_{\text{vac}} \approx 0$). Water auto-refrigerates at the triple point ($T_{tp} = 273.16\,\text{K}, P_{tp} = 611.65\,\text{Pa}$), freezing instantly into an ice plug. Heat load from the suit cooling loop sublimates the ice directly to vacuum:

$$\dot{Q}_{\text{sub}} = \dot{m} \cdot \Delta h_{\text{sub}} = \dot{m} \left( \Delta h_{\text{fus}} + \Delta h_{\text{vap}} \right) \approx \dot{m} \times 2.834 \times 10^6 \ \text{J/kg}$$

Nominal cooling power scales directly with sublimation rate $\dot{m}$. For an astronaut generating $350\,\text{W}$, required mass flow is exactly $\dot{m} = 0.123\,\text{g/s} = 0.444\,\text{kg/hr}$.

2. Young-Laplace Meniscus Anchoring

The liquid-ice interface remains physically anchored within microscopic pore throats ($r_{\text{pore}} \approx 2.5\,\mu\text{m}$) when liquid feed pressure balances capillary curvature and ice shear resistance:

$$\Delta P_{\text{capillary}} = \frac{2\gamma_{\text{SL}} \cos \theta}{r_{\text{pore}}} + \tau_{\text{shear}} \frac{2 \Delta z_{\text{ice}}}{r_{\text{pore}}}$$

If $P_{\text{feed}} > \Delta P_{\text{capillary}}$, liquid shears through the pore throat (Breakthrough). If cooling exceeds incoming liquid enthalpy, the ice sheet grows outward, sealing pores (Freeze Choke).

3. Hertz-Knudsen Vaporization Kinetics

In the space vacuum regime, Knudsen number $\text{Kn} = \lambda / d_{\text{pore}} \gg 10$. Mass effusion from the ice sublimation front follows kinetic gas theory:

$$\dot{m}_{\text{effusion}} = \alpha \cdot A_{\text{pore}} \left( P_{\text{sat}}(T_{\text{ice}}) - P_{\text{vac}} \right) \sqrt{\frac{M_{\text{H}_2\text{O}}}{2\pi R_{\text{u}} T_{\text{ice}}}}$$

Where $\alpha \approx 0.95$ is the sublimation condensation coefficient. Any choking of this free molecular flux shifts operating points instantaneously on the Sentinel phase plane.

$$\mathcal{S}_{\text{anomaly}} = \nabla \Phi(P, \dot{m}) = \left[ \frac{\partial P}{\partial t} \cdot \left( \frac{\partial \dot{m}}{\partial t} \right)^{-1} \right] \implies \text{Breakthrough: } \left(\frac{\partial P}{\partial t} > 0 \;\land\; \frac{\partial \dot{m}}{\partial t} \to 0\right)$$
